Members & roles

Roles

  • Member: profile, propose projects, join projects, sign up to events.
  • Committee: everything a member can do, plus review submissions before they go live and post announcements.
  • Admin: everything the committee can do, plus manage roles, reset passwords, and suspend or delete profiles on this page.

Actions

  • Suspend: blocks sign-in and hides the person from the hub, but keeps their data. Reactivate at any time.
  • Delete: removes them permanently.
